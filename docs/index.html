The Core Idea

Two states. One emotional arc.

Every session delivers tension → crisis → relief → growth. The frantic sector and the serene Dream Warren aren't two games — they're one feeling, split in half.

THE SECTOR

Frantic Stabilisation

  • ⚓ Activate anchors under pressure — holds are interruptible by any hit
  • ⛩ Place Torii Gates (max 2) to block the Drift
  • ⚔ Survive Swarmers, Breakers and Phantoms
  • ⚡ One bar — Mental Guard — drains constantly
  • 📉 Below 30 MGS: Reality Fade — input lag, ghosting, muffled audio, chromatic aberration
DREAM WARREN

Beautiful Recovery

  • 🐇 Tap Lume-Bunnies: +5 MGS each, satisfying pop
  • 🛡 Stand near Rams the Guardian for passive regen
  • ☁️ Zero enemies. Zero threat. Pure calm
  • 💎 Earn Resonance Fragments for upgrades & cosmetics
  • ✨ Collapse is not a game over — it's the reward

Systems

Built for 2–4 minute sessions

One input = one action. The difficulty comes from decisions under pressure, not control complexity.

⚓

Anchor System

3–5 anchors per sector. Hold to channel (1.5–3s). Any damage interrupts. Complete all to stabilise the sector.

⛩

Box Protocol

Torii Gates block enemies and slow the Drift. Max 2 active, 8s shared cooldown, 3 hits from Breakers.

⚔

Three Enemies

Swarmers rush (fast, weak). Breakers siege anchors (slow, heavy). Phantoms phase unpredictably. Depth through behaviour, not count.

📉

Reality Fade

Below 30 MGS the world breaks: 100–300ms input delay, ghost frames, RGB split, low-pass audio, flickering UI.

🐇

Dream Warren

Collapse triggers a 20–40s sanctuary. Collect bunnies, rest by the guardian, restore to 70–100 MGS.

💎

Ethical Progression

Resonance Fragments buy upgrades & cosmetics. Never sell power. Monetise wanting to look cooler, not needing to win.
